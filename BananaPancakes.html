🍌 Why spotty bananas?

As bananas ripen, enzymes turn starch into sugars and break down cell walls. That makes them sweeter, softer and easy to mash. Dark peel spots often show ripeness—not sugar. Avoid mouldy or rotten fruit.

Teacher notes

One batch: 4 regular ripe bananas (about 400 g peeled), one 150 g Aji cheese-mix packet, 25 ml fresh milk to start and 5 g butter for the pan. Use 30 ml batter per pancake (two level 15 ml tablespoons).

Check the batter: if too thick, add 25 ml milk, whisk and check again. The optional 25–100 ml total is a range, not a target. Do not add more milk to runny batter. These teacher-selected proportions are an untested adaptation, not the manufacturer’s recipe. Cook a small test pancake first; more dry mix or fewer bananas may be needed if it will not hold together. The animation shows a texture goal, not a guaranteed result.

Ingredients: the Aji mix already contains wheat flour, egg and milk. No separate egg or flour is included in this demonstration. Each group making a batch needs its own packet and four bananas.

Safety: an adult supports hot-pan work. Wash hands; never taste raw batter. The mix contains wheat/gluten, egg and milk; butter and fresh milk contain milk. Check allergies and avoid cross-contact. Dark peel spots often accompany ripening, but fruit with mould or a rotten smell should be discarded. Animation times are not real cooking timers. Check the pancake centre is cooked, then cool before tasting.
