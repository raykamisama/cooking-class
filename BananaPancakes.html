Teacher guide & recipe notes

One four-banana batch: 4 medium ripe bananas (about 400 g after peeling), 150 g Aji cheese pancake mix (one packet), fresh milk kept aside in a 100 ml measuring cup (optional), and about 5 g butter to lightly grease a nonstick pan. Scoop 30 ml batter per small pancake using 2 level 15 ml tablespoons or a marked measuring cup. This is one demonstration batch, with no per-student or per-group repetition.

Milk is not a fixed 100 ml ingredient here. With four regular bananas and one 150 g packet, I cannot verify 100 ml milk as the correct amount. The bananas already add moisture. The demonstration therefore begins with 0 ml added milk. Reserve 100 ml in a measuring cup; after whisking, add 25 ml only if the real batter needs thinning, then whisk and check again. The board tracks only the milk actually added. It is fine to leave the milk out. Do not add all 100 ml merely because it is measured.

This is an untested, banana-heavy adaptation, not the manufacturer's plain-pancake recipe. Four regular bananas vary in weight and moisture; the animation shows a target texture, not proof that these proportions will cook or flip successfully. Cook one small test pancake before class. If the real mixture is already runny, add no milk. If the test pancake will not hold together, the dry-mix/banana balance needs changing; the simulator cannot validate a fixed recipe. Each group making its own batch uses one 150 g packet, not three.

No separate egg or flour is needed: your Aji packet already contains wheat flour, egg and milk. We are using fresh milk as the liquid for this banana adaptation. Plain flour with fresh eggs would need a different recipe. Use fresh milk as requested. Keep the sugar aside. Butter is only a thin coating on the pan, not a pool of frying fat.

  1. Peel all four bananas.
  2. Put the four whole peeled bananas into the bowl.
  3. Use the masher three times. Watch the whole fruit become chunks, then mash.
  4. Add one 150 g packet of mix. Reserve the milk; do not pour it in automatically.
  5. Use the balloon whisk for three slow circles. Check the real batter. If it needs thinning, add 25 ml of the reserved fresh milk, whisk and check again. Do not add milk to an already thin mixture.
  6. Heat a nonstick pan on low, then spread a thin coating of butter. If butter browns or smokes, lower the heat and let the pan cool before continuing.
  7. Pour 30 ml batter for one small pancake. Wait until bubbles appear and the edges set, then flip. Cook until the centre is set; a browned outside alone does not prove it is cooked.
  8. Move to a clean plate, cool and check the temperature before tasting.

Safety and allergies: an adult supports real pan work and handles the heat when needed. Wash hands and equipment. Do not taste raw batter. The mix contains wheat/gluten, egg and milk; butter also contains milk. Check the actual packet and student allergies, including banana, and prevent cross-contact. Use spotty bananas only when they have no mould or rotten smell. The on-screen heating and cooking waits are animations, not real cooking timers.
