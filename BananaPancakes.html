Teacher guide & recipe notes

For 15 students: use your three 150 g packets of Ajinomoto Aji cheese pancake mix (450 g total), room-temperature water in the amount printed on each packet, and about 5 medium ripe bananas (roughly one third of a banana each). Aim for at least 15 small pancakes. Yield depends on pancake size. Divide batter equally among groups and cook a small portion for each student. The class-size setting changes turn-taking, not these recipe quantities.

Packet recipe: the mix already contains egg and milk. Add only the water specified on the packet; do not add your eggs, milk, sugar or butter. The photo's water figure is not clear enough to transcribe reliably, so read that amount directly from your packet before measuring. Mix until combined. Keep mashed banana separate as a topping, so the packet's batter ratio stays unchanged.

  1. Peel the bananas with clean hands.
  2. Mash them with a fork in a clean bowl. Cover and set aside.
  3. Measure the packet's room-temperature water and add it to the pancake mix.
  4. Stir gently until combined.
  5. With the teacher beside the student, heat a nonstick pan according to the packet, then cook on low heat. The packet describes preheating for 1 minute and cooling the pan briefly on a damp cloth; the teacher handles moving the hot pan. Pour small pancakes. When the edges set and bubbles appear, flip once. The packet suggests about 1½ minutes more; check the centre is fully cooked rather than relying on the simulation.
  6. Transfer to a clean plate. Let cool, check the temperature, then spoon mashed banana on top and serve.

Student cooking: you support each student. Stay beside the pan, keep its handle turned inward and use a long spatula. Only a student who can use the equipment safely should work at the pan; you take over whenever needed. The screen gives every student a cooking turn, but does not grant permission to handle heat. Use a fork for mashing; no knife is needed. Wash hands and equipment before the lesson. Never taste raw batter. Use spotty ripe bananas only if there is no mould or rotten smell.

Allergies: the cheese mix contains wheat/gluten, egg and milk; check the actual packet for the full ingredient and allergen list. Also check banana allergies and avoid cross-contact. This recipe is not suitable for students allergic to those ingredients.
